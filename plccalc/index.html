1. Pendahuluan dan Ruang Lingkup

PLC Calc ("Aplikasi") adalah aplikasi kalkulator programmer otomasi industri (konversi heksadesimal, desimal, biner, oktal, bilangan pecahan IEEE-754, BCD, urutan byte, serta manipulasi bit register), kalkulator skala analog PLC & drive (0..27648, 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, PROFIdrive 16#4000), kalkulator sensor temperatur industri (RTD Platina IEC 60751 & Termokopel NIST ITS-90), serta alat bantu instrumentasi lapangan (kalibrasi As-Found / As-Left dengan ekspor laporan PDF/Excel, diagnosa loop NAMUR NE 43, dan kalkulator level tekanan diferensial / DP Level) yang dikembangkan oleh ControlEngineerID ("kami").

Kebijakan Privasi ini menjelaskan bagaimana informasi dikelola saat Anda menggunakan Aplikasi, termasuk integrasi Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) untuk menampilkan iklan video berhadiah (rewarded ads), Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK untuk pengelolaan persetujuan privasi, dan Google Play Billing untuk pembelian dalam aplikasi (in-app purchases).

2. Data yang Diproses Secara Lokal di Perangkat Anda (Tanpa Akun & Tanpa Server Pengembang)

  • Tanpa Akun Pengguna: PLC Calc tidak memiliki fitur pendaftaran, pembuatan akun, maupun proses masuk (login).
  • Tanpa Server Pengembang: Kami tidak mengoperasikan server backend milik pengembang untuk mengumpulkan, menyimpan, atau menerima data pribadi maupun riwayat perhitungan teknis Anda.
  • Penyimpanan Lokal di Perangkat (Jetpack DataStore): Seluruh angka masukan, nilai register, slot memori konverter (saved_memory), konfigurasi sensor tersimpan (saved_sensors), hasil uji linieritas lapangan, nama teknisi (report_technician) dan nama perusahaan (report_company) yang Anda ketik pada kop laporan, serta waktu mulai masa percobaan Pro 3 hari (trial_start_millis) dan status akses Pro (pro_lifetime, rewarded_until_millis) disimpan semata-mata secara lokal di penyimpanan internal Aplikasi pada perangkat Anda (plccalc_prefs).
  • Ekspor Laporan PDF & Excel (FileProvider): Saat Anda memilih mengekspor laporan kalibrasi atau pembacaan sensor ke file PDF atau Excel (.xlsx), file tersebut dibuat secara lokal di direktori cache internal Aplikasi (cacheDir/reports/) dan hanya dibagikan ke aplikasi lain di perangkat Anda apabila Anda secara eksplisit memilih tindakan bagikan (Share melalui Android FileProvider). Kami tidak pernah menerima salinan laporan tersebut.
  • Penghapusan Data Lokal: Menghapus instalan Aplikasi (uninstall) atau memilih "Hapus Data" (Clear Storage) pada pengaturan sistem Android akan menghapus seluruh data lokal tersebut secara permanen dari perangkat Anda.

3. Izin Aplikasi (Android Permissions)

Aplikasi hanya mendeklarasikan dua izin standar di AndroidManifest.xml:

  1. android.permission.INTERNET — Digunakan hanya oleh SDK resmi Google (Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, dan Google UMP) untuk memuat iklan berhadiah, menampilkan formulir persetujuan privasi, dan memverifikasi transaksi pembelian dengan layanan Google Play. Seluruh fitur kalkulasi teknik bekerja secara offline.
  2. android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Digunakan oleh SDK Google untuk memeriksa apakah koneksi jaringan tersedia sebelum memuat iklan atau memperbarui informasi persetujuan.

Aplikasi tidak meminta izin lokasi GPS (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), kamera, mikrofon, kontak, telepon, maupun akses penyimpanan eksternal.

4. Data yang Dikumpulkan oleh SDK Pihak Ketiga (Google)

Meskipun kode aplikasi ControlEngineerID tidak mengirimkan data ke server kami sendiri, Aplikasi menyertakan Software Development Kit (SDK) resmi dari Google untuk menyediakan fitur iklan berhadiah dan pembelian Pro. SDK ini secara otomatis mengumpulkan dan mengirimkan data tertentu ke Google.

A. Google AdMob (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads)

Aplikasi menggunakan Google AdMob untuk menayangkan iklan video berhadiah (rewarded ads) ketika pengguna memilih menonton iklan guna membuka fitur Pro selama 24 jam. Aplikasi mengaktifkan DELAY_APP_MEASUREMENT_INIT = true pada manifest sehingga inisialisasi pengukuran iklan ditunda hingga status persetujuan (UMP) diperiksa.

Berdasarkan dokumentasi resmi Google (Google Play data disclosure for Google Mobile Ads SDK), Google Mobile Ads SDK mengumpulkan dan membagikan jenis data berikut secara otomatis untuk tujuan periklanan/pemasaran (advertising or marketing), analitik (analytics), dan pencegahan penipuan/keamanan (fraud prevention, security, and compliance):

  1. Alamat IP (Lokasi Perkiraan / Approximate Location): Mengumpulkan alamat IP perangkat yang dapat digunakan untuk memperkirakan lokasi umum (perkiraan kota/wilayah) perangkat.
  2. Interaksi Produk Pengguna (User Product Interactions / App Activity): Mengumpulkan interaksi pengguna di dalam Aplikasi seperti pembukaan aplikasi (app launch), ketukan (taps), dan penayangan video iklan.
  3. Informasi Diagnostik (Diagnostic Information / App Info and Performance): Mengumpulkan informasi performa Aplikasi dan SDK, termasuk waktu muat aplikasi (app launch time), tingkat macet (hang rate), log kerusakan (crash logs), dan penggunaan energi.
  4. Pengidentifikasi Perangkat dan Akun (Device and Account Identifiers): Mengumpulkan ID Iklan Android (Android Advertising ID / AdID), app set ID, serta—apabila relevan—pengidentifikasi lain yang terkait dengan akun yang masuk pada perangkat.

Seluruh data pengguna yang dikumpulkan oleh Google Mobile Ads SDK dienkripsi selama pengiriman (encrypted in transit) menggunakan protokol Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

1. Introduction and Scope

PLC Calc ("the App") is an industrial automation programmer calculator (hexadecimal, decimal, binary, octal, IEEE-754 floating-point, BCD, byte order, and register bit manipulation), PLC analog & drive scaling utility (0..27648, 4–20 mA, 0–10 V, PROFIdrive 16#4000), industrial temperature sensor calculator (IEC 60751 Platinum RTDs & NIST ITS-90 Thermocouples), and field instrumentation toolkit (As-Found / As-Left calibration with PDF/Excel report export, NAMUR NE 43 loop diagnostics, and differential pressure / DP level calculator) developed by ControlEngineerID ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App, including its integration of Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for rewarded video advertisements, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK for privacy consent management, and Google Play Billing for optional in-app purchases.

2. Data Processed Locally on Your Device (No User Accounts & No Developer Server)

  • No User Accounts: PLC Calc does not require or offer user registration, login, or accounts.
  • No Developer Backend Server: We do not operate any backend server that collects, stores, or receives your personal data or engineering calculations.
  • Local On-Device Storage (Jetpack DataStore): All input numbers, register states, converter memory slots (saved_memory), saved sensor configurations (saved_sensors), field linearity test samples, optional technician name (report_technician) and company name (report_company) entered for report headers, as well as the 3-day Pro trial start timestamp (trial_start_millis) and Pro entitlement status (pro_lifetime, rewarded_until_millis) are stored strictly locally in the App's internal storage on your device (plccalc_prefs).
  • PDF & Excel Report Export (FileProvider): When you export a calibration or sensor report as a PDF or Excel (.xlsx) file, the file is generated locally in the App's private cache directory (cacheDir/reports/) and is shared with other apps on your device only when you explicitly invoke the Android share sheet via androidx.core.content.FileProvider. We never receive copies of your generated reports.
  • Deleting Local Data: Uninstalling the App or clearing the App's storage in your Android system settings permanently deletes all locally stored data from your device.

3. Application Permissions

The App declares only two standard permissions in AndroidManifest.xml:

  1. android.permission.INTERNET — Used strictly by official Google SDKs (Google Play Billing, Google Mobile Ads / AdMob, and Google UMP) to load rewarded ads, display consent forms, and verify in-app purchases with Google Play. All engineering calculators work offline.
  2. android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Used by Google SDKs to check network connectivity before requesting ads or consent updates.

The App does not request GPS location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), camera, microphone, contacts, phone state, or external storage permissions.

4. Data Collected by Third-Party SDKs (Google)

Although ControlEngineerID's own application code does not transmit data to any developer server, the App integrates official Google SDKs to support rewarded advertising and in-app purchases. These SDKs automatically collect and share certain data with Google.

A. Google AdMob (com.google.android.gms:play-services-ads)

We use Google AdMob to serve rewarded video ads when users choose to watch a video ad to unlock Pro features for 24 hours. The App sets DELAY_APP_MEASUREMENT_INIT = true in the manifest so measurement initialization is delayed until UMP consent status is verified.

According to Google's official documentation (Google Play data disclosure for Google Mobile Ads SDK), the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following data types for advertising or marketing, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes:

  1. IP Address (Approximate Location): Collects the device's IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device.
  2. User Product Interactions (App Activity): Collects user product interactions within the App, including app launches, taps, and video views (such as rewarded ad views).
  3. Diagnostic Information (App Info and Performance): Collects information related to the performance of the App and the SDK, including app launch time, hang rate, crash logs/diagnostics, and energy usage.
  4. Device and Account Identifiers: Collects the Android advertising ID (AdID), app set ID, and, if applicable, other identifiers related to signed-in accounts on the device.

All user data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using the Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) protocol.

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please review:
• Google's Partner Sites Policy
• Google Privacy Policy

B. Google Play Billing (com.android.billingclient:billing-ktx)

We use Google Play's billing system to offer the optional one-time lifetime unlock (pro_lifetime).

  • All payment transactions and payment methods (credit cards, debit cards, carrier billing, e-wallets, or Google Play balance) are processed directly and exclusively by Google Play via the Google Play Store app on your device.
  • ControlEngineerID never receives, accesses, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address.
  • The App only queries the local Google Play Store service on your device to check product availability and verify purchase entitlement tokens (PurchaseToken) to activate pro_lifetime locally.

5. Advertising Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland — UMP SDK) and User Controls

  • UMP Consent Form: The App uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK (com.google.android.ump:user-messaging-platform) to present a certified consent form to users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom (UK), and Switzerland in accordance with the GDPR and the Google EU User Consent Policy before serving ads.
  • Privacy Options Entry Point: Where required by regional privacy regulations (PrivacyOptionsRequirementStatus.REQUIRED), a Privacy options button is displayed in the App's top bar, allowing you to review, modify, or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Android Advertising ID Controls: On any Android device, you can reset or delete your Android Advertising ID at any time through your device's system settings (Settings > Google > All services > Ads or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

6. Data Security and Retention

  • All network traffic transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing Library is encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Because ControlEngineerID does not maintain a backend server or user database, we do not store personal data on our own infrastructure. Data collected by Google SDKs is retained and managed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

PLC Calc is a specialized technical utility designed for professional engineers, industrial technicians, and engineering students. The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
