Bahasa Indonesia

1. Ringkasan Singkat

TV Remote untuk Smart TV dirancang dengan prinsip kendali perangkat lokal (local-first) serta transparansi monetisasi:

  • Tidak ada akun pengguna atau pendaftaran milik pengembang.
  • Kendali TV langsung di jaringan lokal (LAN/Wi-Fi): seluruh perintah kendali TV berlangsung secara langsung antara ponsel Anda dan perangkat TV di jaringan lokal (LAN/Wi-Fi) yang sama.
  • Model Akses Berbasis Durasi (Jujur & Transparan): Coba gratis 7 hari. Setelah itu tonton iklan untuk 1 jam, atau berlangganan mulai Rp2.000/hari. Power, Volume, dan Mute selalu gratis. Saat akses aktif, seluruh tombol, fitur (keyboard, pintasan aplikasi, numpad, multi-TV), dan semua tata letak (termasuk Plus) terbuka penuh.
  • ID Perangkat (Hash Satu Arah) untuk Trial 7 Hari: ID perangkat (hash satu arah) dikirim ke server kami hanya untuk mencegah trial diulang. Aplikasi menghitung nilai hash satu arah SHA-256(ANDROID_ID + ":" + packageName + ":" + SALT_APLIKASI) (64 karakter hex) dan mengirimkannya ke server Cloudflare Workers kami semata-mata untuk mencatat waktu mulai trial (startedAtServer). Kami tidak pernah mengirim ANDROID_ID mentah, tidak menyimpan alamat IP, tidak memakai cookie, dan tidak membagikan hash tersebut kepada pihak ketiga mana pun.
  • Pembelian Dalam Aplikasi & Langganan (Google Play Billing): Paket prabayar/harian, mingguan, langganan bulanan/tahunan, atau sekali beli selamanya (Lifetime) diproses secara aman oleh Google Play Billing.
  • Iklan Rewarded Opsional (Google AdMob & UMP): Aplikasi tidak menampilkan iklan banner atau iklan pop-up/interstisial paksa. Iklan video Rewarded dari Google AdMob hanya dimuat dan ditampilkan apabila pengguna memilih untuk menonton 1 iklan guna mendapatkan akses penuh gratis selama 1 jam (dapat ditumpuk hingga maks. 3 jam).

2. Data yang Disimpan Secara Lokal di Perangkat Anda

Agar Anda tidak perlu memasangkan ulang (pairing) TV setiap kali membuka aplikasi dan agar preferensi Anda tersimpan, data berikut disimpan secara lokal di penyimpanan internal pribadi aplikasi (DataStore Preferences dan filesDir yang terenkripsi dengan AndroidKeyStore):

  • Daftar TV Tersimpan & Pengaturan (tvremote_prefs): Nama tampilan TV, alamat IP lokal (mis. 192.168.1.x), port protokol (8002/8001 atau 6466), tipe protokol (SAMSUNG_TIZEN atau ANDROID_TV), alamat MAC Wi-Fi/Ethernet opsional (khusus fitur eksperimental Wake-on-LAN), token pairing lokal TV Samsung Tizen, status pairing Android TV, rute pintasan aplikasi lokal, serta preferensi aplikasi (tata letak, tema warna, getar/haptic, layar tetap menyala, dan masa aktif akses trial/rewarded/pass lokal).
  • Sertifikat Klien Pairing Android TV (atv_client_cert.der & atv_client_key.enc): Pasangan sertifikat X.509v3 self-signed dan kunci privat RSA-2048 yang dibuat secara acak di perangkat Anda untuk otentikasi mutual TLS (mTLS) ke port 6467/6466 Android TV. Kunci privat dienkripsi saat disimpan (at-rest) menggunakan kunci master AES-256-GCM di dalam perangkat keras AndroidKeyStore.
  • Log Diagnosa Sementara di Memori (DiagLog): Ringkasan status koneksi teknis hingga 300 baris terakhir disimpan di RAM (memori sementara, hilang saat aplikasi ditutup). Log ini tidak pernah dikirim otomatis ke mana pun dan hanya disalin ke papan klip (clipboard) ponsel apabila Anda menekan tombol "Salin diagnosa" secara manual.

Seluruh data lokal di atas dapat Anda hapus kapan saja dengan menghapus TV dari daftar di dalam aplikasi, memilih Hapus Data / Clear Storage di pengaturan Android, atau mencopot pemasangan (uninstall) aplikasi.

5. Izin Aplikasi & Penggunaan Jaringan Lokal / Multicast

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Digunakan untuk komunikasi jaringan lokal (LAN) langsung ke alamat IP TV Anda (SSDP/mDNS, WebSocket Samsung Tizen port 8002/8001, mutual TLS Android TV port 6467/6466, dan paket UDP Wake-on-LAN), serta komunikasi HTTPS ke endpoint status trial kami, Google Play Billing, dan Google AdMob/UMP. Atribut android:usesCleartextTraffic="true" diaktifkan karena TV Samsung Tizen model 2016–2017 dan endpoint lokal (port 8001) di LAN menggunakan HTTP/WebSocket lokal tanpa sertifikat TLS publik.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Memeriksa ketersediaan koneksi jaringan sebelum memulai trial, menghubungkan soket ke TV, atau memuat iklan Rewarded.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — Mengizinkan pembuatan WifiManager.MulticastLock sementara saat menekan tombol "Pindai Wi-Fi" agar ponsel dapat menerima balasan SSDP/UPnP dan mDNS dari TV di jaringan Wi-Fi lokal.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Diperlukan oleh SDK Google Mobile Ads pada Android 13+ untuk mengakses ID Iklan Android saat menayangkan iklan Rewarded opsional.

English

1. Executive Summary

Smart TV Remote Control is built on a local-first hardware control architecture with transparent duration-based access:

  • No user accounts or sign-ups required.
  • Direct local network (LAN/Wi-Fi) TV control: all TV remote commands occur directly between your phone and your TV on your local Wi-Fi network.
  • Duration-Based Access Model (Honest & Transparent): 7-day free trial. After that, watch an ad for 1 hour, or subscribe starting from Rp2,000/day ($0.49/day). Power, Volume, and Mute are always free. While access is active, all buttons, features, and layouts (including Plus) are unlocked.
  • One-Way Hashed Device ID for 7-Day Trial: A one-way hashed device ID is sent to our server solely to prevent repeated free trials. The app computes SHA-256(ANDROID_ID + ":" + packageName + ":" + SALT_APLIKASI) (64 hex characters) and sends only this irreversible hash to our Cloudflare Workers endpoint to record the trial start timestamp (startedAtServer). Raw ANDROID_ID is never sent, IP addresses are never stored, no cookies are used, and the hash is never shared with third parties.
  • In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (Google Play Billing): Daily/weekly prepaid passes, monthly/yearly auto-renewing subscriptions, or a one-time Lifetime purchase are processed securely by Google Play Billing.
  • Optional Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob & UMP): The app contains zero banner ads and zero forced pop-up/interstitial ads. A Rewarded video ad from Google AdMob is only loaded and shown when a user chooses to watch 1 ad to unlock 1 hour of free full access (stackable up to 3 hours).

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

So that you do not have to re-pair your TV every time you open the app, the following information is stored locally on your device within the app's private storage (DataStore Preferences and filesDir protected by AndroidKeyStore):

  • Saved TVs & App Preferences (tvremote_prefs): TV display name, local IP address (e.g., 192.168.1.x), protocol port (8002/8001 or 6466), protocol type (SAMSUNG_TIZEN or ANDROID_TV), optional Wi-Fi/Ethernet MAC address (for experimental Wake-on-LAN), local Samsung Tizen pairing token, Android TV paired flag, local app-shortcut routes, and local preferences (layout, color skin, vibration feedback, keep screen on, and local trial/rewarded/pass expiry timestamps).
  • Android TV Pairing Client Identity (atv_client_cert.der & atv_client_key.enc): A randomly generated self-signed X.509v3 certificate and RSA-2048 private key created on your device for mutual TLS (mTLS) authentication with Android TV ports 6467/6466. The private key is encrypted at rest using an AES-256-GCM master key stored in the hardware-backed AndroidKeyStore.
  • In-Memory Diagnostic Log (DiagLog): A ring buffer of up to 300 lines of recent technical connection events kept only in volatile RAM (cleared when the app closes). It is never transmitted automatically anywhere and is only copied to your system clipboard if you explicitly tap "Copy diagnostics".

You can erase all locally stored data at any time by deleting a TV entry inside the app, clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the application.

3. One-Time 7-Day Trial Verification (Cloudflare Workers + KV)

  • Data Collected: One-way cryptographic hash SHA-256(ANDROID_ID + ":" + packageName + ":" + SALT_APLIKASI) (64 lowercase hex characters). Raw ANDROID_ID is never sent and no other personal data is collected.
  • Purpose: Security and fraud prevention (enforcing one 7-day trial per device).
  • Retention: Retained in Cloudflare Workers KV as deviceHash -> { startedAt } for the operational lifetime of the service to recognize trial usage across reinstalls. IP addresses are never persisted and no cookies are used.
  • Data Sharing: Never shared or sold to any third party.

5. App Permissions & Local Network / Multicast Usage

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used for local network (LAN) communication directly with your TV's IP address (SSDP/mDNS discovery, Samsung Tizen WebSocket on ports 8002/8001, Android TV Remote v2 mutual TLS on ports 6467/6466, and local UDP Wake-on-LAN broadcast), as well as HTTPS communication with our trial verification endpoint, Google Play Billing, and Google AdMob/UMP. android:usesCleartextTraffic="true" is enabled because 2016–2017 Samsung Tizen TVs and local REST/WebSocket endpoints on port 8001 communicate over unencrypted local HTTP/WS on the LAN.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Checks network connectivity status before starting a trial, attempting TV socket connections, or loading Rewarded ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & android.permission.CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE — Acquires a temporary WifiManager.MulticastLock while you tap "Scan Wi-Fi" so the device can receive SSDP/UPnP and mDNS multicast discovery replies from TVs on your local Wi-Fi.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK on Android 13+ to read the Android Advertising ID for opt-in Rewarded ads.
